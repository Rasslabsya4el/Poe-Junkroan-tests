# 2026-09-16 D59. The benchmark's Life floor is 100% of the author's

Status: Accepted

Context: the benchmark floors Life at the author's value with a 3% slack
(0.97), so a build sits at 97% of the author's life and the objective, DPS
with floors, values life above that at zero. The user's reading of run E
found the consequence: Tireless on bleed_bow s3 (1 point adjacent, +4.3%
life, same DPS) and Revenge of the Hunted on poison_spectral_throw s2 (1
point, +5% life, +6.7% EHP) untaken while the point bought a +0.3% DPS leaf.
No hand weight for life above the floor (principle 1), so the choice was
between floors: A) Life at 100% of the author's, B) the author's Life and
TotalEHP, C) keep the slack.

Decision (the user, A): the benchmark's Life floor is the author's life,
no slack. Max-hit floors keep D53's factor, movement keeps its 3% (the
loot's boots reach 1.19x against an author at exactly 1.20x). The search
has no author and keeps the rails' absolute floors; whether those are too
low for the same reason is a rails question, not this one. Proof:
`smoke-frozen` (the 7.1 exit run).
