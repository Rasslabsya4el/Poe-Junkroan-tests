# 2026-09-11 D6. poe.ninja is a calibration benchmark, never a template

Status: Accepted; amended by D6 amd 1

The user wants non-meta discovery. Ninja is used only to detect a broken
optimizer (our archetype much weaker than the ninja median under the same
config). Amended the same day: ninja may also supply *statistics* (how often
a node, mastery or support appears among builds of an archetype) to identify
conventions PoB cannot value, such as brand attachment range. A frequency is
a number, not a build; it enters the objective as a soft constraint.
